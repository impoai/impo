Echo. Capture your day.

Conversations, passing thoughts, ideas out loud. Return to them as transcripts, and give your agent context to remember.

  1. 01

    Capture the moment

    Start Echo for the conversations and spoken thoughts you want to keep. Pause or stop whenever you choose.

  2. 02

    Return to the words

    Browse transcripts in your personal timeline. Jump back to a day and pick up the details.

  3. 03

    Keep the context

    Impo draws useful memories from Echo, so future conversations can start with more of your story.

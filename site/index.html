Echo. Capture your day.

Conversations, passing thoughts, ideas out loud. Return to them as transcripts, and give your agent context to remember.

  1. 01

    Capture the moment

    Start Echo for the conversations and spoken thoughts you want to keep. Pause or stop whenever you choose.

  2. 02

    Return to the words

    Browse transcripts in your personal timeline. Jump back to a day and pick up the details.

  3. 03

    Keep the context

    Impo draws useful memories from Echo, so future conversations can start with more of your story.

Impo Gadgets

Your personal agent.
On your hardware.

Connect a compatible ESP32 or Linux device to Impo. Pair it from your iPhone, then ask your agent to show a message or play a sound.

Explore Gadget SDK ↗
ESP32
Open-source firmware for compatible boards, including the Espressif ESP-SparkBot.
Linux
A device SDK for Raspberry Pi and other Linux devices. Add commands for the hardware you build.

Available commands depend on your hardware and firmware. Gadget setup is currently available in the iOS app.
